Desktop/Shelf Puzzle Calendar

Students craft a 12-sided dodecahedron calendar to learn Platonic solid geometry. Rotating its physical faces builds spatial reasoning while encouraging screen-free, mindful time management thinking.

Learn more

Arts & Crafts

Moonlit Wolf Layered Laser Cutting Wall Art

Students design multi-layer silhouettes via precise laser cutting. They explore light-shadow layering, refine artistic aesthetics and strengthen delicate hands-on crafting abilities in visual art creation.

Learn more

Engineering

Windmill metronome

Using our P Series Models, students assemble swingable windmill blades and rhythm-sync light strips. The project combines mechanical structures, circuit wiring and beat logic for comprehensive engineering training.

Terrain Model

In this project, students will construct laser-cut 3D topographical models to explore physical geography. This engaging activity enhances spatial awareness, making it suitable for Geography or Earth Science courses.

Trusted by Leading Education

Institution

MIT CBA Lists xTool F1 Ultra & Metal Fab in its Inventory
xTool F1 Ultra and Metal Fab systems are listed in MIT Center for Bits and Atoms’ official tool inventory. This recognition validates the research-grade quality and performance of their work for next-generation digital fabrication.
LAUSD Chose xTool P2S Laser Cutter for 300+ Classrooms
LAUSD is implementing the xTool P2S Laser Cutter in over 300 classrooms, launching a teacher training program to maximize its integration. This initiative aims to enrich student creativity and innovation, transforming hands-on learning experiences across the district.
Fab Lab Chose xTool S1 as the Core Laser Cutter for Fab-in-a-Box Initiatives
The Fab Foundation selected xTool S1 as the official laser cutter for its ‘Fab-in-a-Box’ initiative – a turnkey digital fabrication solution developed with Dassault Systèmes SolidWorks. This program expands global access to fab labs, empowering communities with industry-standard tools.
CWRU Selects xTool P2 for Makerspace
Case Western Reserve University (CWRU) has selected the xTool P2 for its innovative makerspace, enhancing hands-on learning and creativity among students. This vibrant makerspace community encourages teamwork and critical thinking, preparing students for future challenges.
