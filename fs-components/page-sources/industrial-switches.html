Increase Productivity with FS Industrial Switches

Featuring an IP41 rating, anti-electromagnetic interference, and microsecond-level latency, FS ruggedized industrial switches are purpose-built for harsh environments to help you digitize operations, protect against cyber threats, and reduce downtime.

Diverse Port Configuration

Support uplinks with speeds ranging from 100M to 10G to accommodate different network architecture layers and ensure scalability for industrial applications.

Proven Industrial Durability

Validated through 7 environmental durability tests, FS industrial switches operate reliably under vibration, shock, dust, extreme temperatures, and strong EMI.

Sub-10ms Recovery

Leverage ERPS, MRP, VRRP, and hardware bypass to achieve sub-10ms failover convergence and comprehensive fault-link recovery, ensuring uninterrupted network connectivity.

TSN-Ready Performance

Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN) prioritizes critical traffic to guarantee real-time communication and minimize latency across industrial control networks.

Secure Network Management

Offer intuitive Web/CLI/SNMP with SSH2.0, SSL, and SNMPv3 encryption, ensuring secure access and efficient network management.
