Lenovo Yoga is a premium ecosystem of laptops, desktops, tablets, and accessories designed around performance, mobility, and versatility. While known for its signature 2-in-1 convertible laptops, Yoga represents a broader portfolio spanning multiple form factors to support different ways of working, creating, and consuming content. This includes clamshell laptops for traditional productivity, convertibles for flexible use, dual-screen designs such as Yoga Book for creative workflows, and All-in-One PCs for streamlined desktop setups. Yoga devices are built to support flexible work, creativity, entertainment, and everyday productivity through features such as high-quality displays, advanced processors, and adaptable designs. The core Yoga lineup includes Yoga Pro, Yoga Slim, Yoga 2-in-1, Yoga Book and Yoga All-in-One products, covering everything from ultra-portable productivity to high-performance computing.

Within the Lenovo portfolio, all laptops are designed to meet performance and reliability standards. Yoga laptops differentiate themselves through their premium design, versatility, and focus on refined user experiences. While many Yoga products are known for their flexible 2-in-1 form factors, featuring 360-degree hinges, touchscreen displays, and pen support, the lineup also includes ultra-slim clamshell and performance-focused devices. This broader approach allows Yoga devices to support a wide range of use cases, from productivity and creativity to entertainment and everyday computing. In addition to versatility, Yoga laptops emphasize high-quality materials, sleek aesthetics, and advanced display technologies, making them suitable for users who want a balance of style, flexibility, and performance
