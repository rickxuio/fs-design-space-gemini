FAQ

F2 Ultra UV delivers an all-in-one, commercial-grade solution for high-speed, crack-free glass customization. It supports 3D inner engraving, fine surface engraving, embossing, and precision cutting without messy pre-treatment or cleaning.

It engraves glass, crystal, acrylic, wood, metal, leather, plastic, rubber, silicone, and ceramic. It also cuts glass, plastics (PE, PP, EVA, PET), fabric, leather, non-transparent acrylic, paper, and wood with burn-free cold processing.

With speeds up to 15,000 mm/s (590.55 ips), dual 48MP cameras, and support for conveyor expansion (200 x 500 mm) as well as rotary attachments up to 150 mm diameter, it streamlines scalable batch orders.

F2 Ultra UV works seamlessly with xTool Studio and LightBurn on Windows and macOS via USB, Wi-Fi, or IP. It supports 2D formats (SVG, DXF, JPG, PNG, BMP, WEBP) and 3D formats (STL, OBJ, AMF, 3MF, GLB, PLY) with one-click 2D-to-3D conversion.

Unlike CO2 lasers that rely on heat and often cause burn marks or micro-cracks on delicate glass and wood, the 355 nm UV laser uses cold photochemical processing with a 0.02 x 0.02 mm spot for smooth, frosty, zero-charring results.
