Simplified Optical Ethernet Solution for Higher Education

Providing an All-optical Integrated Ethernet Solution with Unified Authentication.

Overview Challenges Benefits Solution Customer Stories Related Products Related Resources

Overview

With 20+ years of history and innovation in education, Ruijie's Simplified Optical Ethernet (SOE) solution establishes a next-generation campus network, leveraging all-optical architecture and Ethernet switches for high-speed data transfer. By integrating Layer 3 switches, this solution contributes to smart campus networks by enhancing routing and network segmentation for optimized traffic management. With intelligent management, it seamlessly integrates IoT, wired, and wireless O&M, meeting the digital transformation needs of higher education institutions. Scenario-based innovations simplify O&M, and SAM+ enhances authentication security, ensuring a future-ready intelligent campus.

Challenges

Complex Scenarios & Poor Performance

As teaching modes evolve and the number of terminals rapidly grows, the traffic demand on campus network devices has increased sharply. However, the traditional network architecture falls short of meeting users' urgent needs for flexible network access and ultra-high bandwidth.

Common Authentication Challenges

The campus network authentication faces challenges in security, unified authentication, user experience, and operation efficiency.

Difficult O&M

With the rapid development of information technology, colleges and universities are increasingly embracing digital and intelligent network construction. However, traditional methods such as human inspection and manual operations are becoming insufficient to support the stable operation of campus networks.

Benefits

Full Coverage High Bandwidth

The all-optical campus network construction encompasses diverse scenarios such as smart classroom, classroom, office, dormitory, stadium and outdoor areas. The solution supports all-optical access, ensuring flexibility and scalability with sufficient bandwidth.

Secure & Reliable Authentication

Our simplified network authentication ensures a superior user experience by integrating access and egress, thereby providing a secure and reliable authentication experience.

Simplified O&M

The SOE solution supports full-scenario network O&M management, including visualized O&M, fault location, and plug-and-play, making O&M simple and efficient for smart campus network solutions.
