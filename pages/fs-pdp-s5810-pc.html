48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 PoE+ Switch, 48 x PoE+ Ports @740W, with 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG & Perpetual PoE, Broadcom Chip

The S5810-48TS-P simplifies access layer deployment for enterprise, education, and healthcare networks with 48 x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ ports, up to 30W per port, 740W PoE budget, and 4 x 1G/10G SFP+ uplinks. Perpetual PoE keeps connected devices powered during switch reboots for uninterrupted network services.

Running on PicOS® with a perpetual license, it supports OpenFlow, MLAG, OSPF, BGP, IPv4/IPv6, PBR, PTP and STP without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

PicOS® Ensures Stable and Secure Network Operations

PicOS® features a modular design for independent component operation, a dual-partition rollback for quick restoration, comprehensive L2/L3 features, and various security authentication methods to enhance network reliability and availability.

MLAG Eliminates Bottlenecks and Realizes Uninterrupted Services

MLAG delivers device-level link redundancy and balancing by aggregating links across two switches, boosting network bandwidth, reliability, and availability, ensuring uninterrupted operation of critical services.

1/10G Ports with Non-blocking Line-Rate for Versatile Networking

48x 10/100/1000Mbps auto-sensing RJ45 ports adapt to multiple devices' seamless connections. The 10G SFP+ uplink ports enhance flexibility for connecting to core networks or aggregation switches, optimizing network scalability and performance.
