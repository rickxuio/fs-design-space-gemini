Why Choose Custom Cable Assemblies From ADI?

Save time and money

Our pre-terminated assemblies are engineered to help you reduce installation costs by up to 50% compared to field-terminated cables. Because the cables are pre-tested and ready to install, you eliminate the need for field testing equipment and excess labor.

Minimize waste

Our custom assemblies arrive in precise lengths and configurations, reducing material overages and trial-and-error cutting. That means no unnecessary waste and costs.

Ensure reliable, high-performance connections

Each cable is factory-polished and performance-tested before it leaves the facility. You can count on consistent quality and reduced risk of failure in your installations.

Accelerate project timelines

Whether you're working in a fast-paced data center or a critical infrastructure deployment, custom assemblies help speed up deployment and reduce on-site installation time.

Support for complex installations

Ideal for critical environments where performance and uptime are non-negotiable, including military, healthcare, telecommunications, utilities and data centers, our custom cable assemblies are built to meet rigorous standards.

What Sets ADI Apart?

When you work with ADI, you gain access to more than just products. Our dedicated team of RCDD-accredited sales engineers offer end-to-end support on your custom cabling projects. We'll review your plans and specifications to build the right solution and ensure high-quality results at competitive prices. Plus, our expert support is included at no additional cost to you.
