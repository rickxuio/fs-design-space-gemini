The New Membership Program: Growth Meets Rewards

Elevate your membership tiers with the Growth System. Fuel your Off-Grid rewards with the Rewards System.

New Membership

Elite tiers are purely driven by your cumulative Renogy Miles via the Growth System, entirely separated from spendable assets.

Spend your Renogy Rays freely within the Rewards System without ever lowering your tier status or losing Miles-driven perks.

Enjoy guaranteed monthly coupons and exclusive access to periodic member events.

Steady daily actions build your Miles, while hitting major off-grid milestones unlocks high-value Rays.

Legacy Membership

Tiers were determined by your active Rays balance, mixing growth tracking with spendable points.

Redeeming Rays for rewards directly reduced your balance, which could lower your tier level.

Perks were mostly limited to one-time coupons, lacking predictable, recurring rewards.

Supported solar and app tracking, but Rays were rewarded within a fragmented, unstructured framework.

FAQ

Answers to common questions across all six categories of the Renogy Membership Program, Miles tiers, Rays Marketplace, and policies.

The new Renogy Membership Program is an advanced ecosystem tailored for our dedicated community of Off-Grid solar users and builders. We upgraded the legacy structure to separate your long-term loyalty standing from your everyday spendable rewards via a dual-system structure: Growth System (Renogy Miles) for tier standing and Rewards System (Renogy Rays) for spendable currency.

The legacy program relied on a single point pool where spending Rays accidentally downgraded your membership level. The new dual structure separates Renogy Miles (which determine your tier, can never be spent, and expire only after a fixed 2-year rolling window) from Renogy Rays (spendable digital currency whose redemption has zero impact on your tier).

Registration and enrollment in the Renogy Membership Program are completely free. Any user who creates a verified account on our official online store or Renogy App is instantly enrolled.

Yes, absolutely. Renogy uses a Single Sign-On (SSO) unified account structure so your membership profile, active tier standing, accumulated Renogy Miles, and spendable Renogy Rays synchronize in real-time across the official online store, Renogy App, and Renogy Forum.

No. Renogy Miles and Renogy Rays are strictly non-transferable and tied exclusively to the unique account that performed the initial transaction or engagement task.

The Growth System tracks your long-term loyalty using Renogy Miles. Your cumulative Miles determine your membership level from L1 Adventurer (Join for Free) up to L5 Trailblazer (100,001+ Miles).

Log in to your unified Renogy account on the official online store or open the Membership Center in the Renogy App to view your real-time tier level, cumulative Miles, and progress to the next level.

Earn Miles through official store purchases, App Download & Login (+200), Profile Completion (+300), Daily Check-ins (+10), Community Posts & Milestones, Newsletter Subscriptions, and Smart Solar Power Generation (1 kWh = 1 Mile, max 50/day).

Renogy Miles are never deducted when you redeem rewards and remain valid across a fixed 2-year rolling window to protect your membership tier.

Renogy Rays are your spendable digital currency earned from purchases ($1 = 10 to 15 Rays depending on tier), check-in streaks, profile completion, and special milestones. Spend them freely in the Rewards Marketplace.

You can redeem Rays for US store coupons ($10 to $330 OFF), gift cards ($5 to $50), and direct product exchanges including DC circuit breaker boxes, solar combiner boxes, temperature sensors, and charge controllers.

Once Renogy Rays are redeemed for a coupon, gift card, or product exchange voucher, the transaction is final and redeemed Rays are non-refundable.

If an order is canceled or refunded, the corresponding Miles and Rays credited from that purchase are deducted from your account balance.

Redeemed Rays and single-use reward vouchers applied to a canceled or refunded order are generally non-refundable once processed.

Simulating, tampering with, or uploading fabricated solar generation data violates program rules and will result in reversal of illegitimate Miles/Rays and potential account suspension.

Spam, duplicate posts, or inappropriate content will be removed, associated community Miles/Rays revoked, and repeat violations may restrict community and membership privileges.

Abnormal patterns such as high-frequency ordering and returning to farm points trigger automated risk controls, leading to point clawbacks and membership review.

Creating multiple accounts, automated scripts, or bulk registrations to abuse Welcome Bundles or referral/mission rewards is strictly prohibited and subject to account termination.
